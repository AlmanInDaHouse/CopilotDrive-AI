repository and diff checks. Instrument directly only on named disposable AVDs;
preserve the valuable AVD package/DataStore fingerprints and return to it afterwards.

timeout and recovery, callback-only pending, negative human result, policy-stage privacy,
orchestrator error attribution, and Compose acoustic controls without real audio.

then one disposable attempt and a justified reversible alternative. Keep A/B separate.

hardware/consent; absent hardware keeps PENDING. No provider or microphone CI calls.
